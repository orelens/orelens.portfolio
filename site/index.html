Process

How a month with Orelens works

  1. 01

    Message us

    Tell us the brand, what you sell, and where you post. WhatsApp and email both reach Nikunj.

  2. 02

    Align on the brief

    Before a colour is chosen, we ask what the piece has to do, who it is for, and where it will run.

  3. 03

    Pick a plan

    Focus, Frame or Full Spectrum for brands. Backstage if you are an agency. Scope and price are confirmed in writing.

  4. 04

    Deliver on the dates we set

    You send requests. We deliver on the dates we confirmed, and we flag a slip before it lands on you.

Values

Six promises you can check

These are about how your work is handled. They are not a slogan wall.

See the business first

Before we pick a colour, we ask what the post has to sell, who it is for and where it will run. The brief comes before the brush.

Sharp in every frame

Every static, slide and cut gets a final check for spelling, safe zones, crop, audio levels and brand colours. Small details are the job.

Own the timeline

We confirm dates when we take the work and flag delays before they happen. If we slip, we say so and fix it.

Honest by default

We show only work we actually did. Concept pieces are labelled as concept. We say no to work we cannot do well, and we are clear about what a package includes.

One brand, every post

We keep your fonts, colours and voice consistent month after month, so the feed looks like one brand.

Keep refocusing

Every month we review what worked, adjust and suggest the next idea. We use new tools as helpers, and we do not treat them as a shortcut on quality.

Business first · Sharp details · On time · Honest proof · Consistent brand · Always improving

FAQ

Straight answers

Where is Orelens based?

Bhopal, India. We work with brands in India and with agencies in the USA and Dubai.

How do we start?

Chat on WhatsApp at @nikunj.sonii, or email graphicdesigner.nikunj@gmail.com. Say what you sell and what you need this month.

Why does every plan say pricing on request?

The fee depends on the plan and the volume. We confirm it in writing before you commit. This site does not list a price.

What is included, and what is discussed separately?

Each plan describes the kind of work. The exact count of posts, reels, revisions and the turnaround are written into your scope. Stock licences, paid fonts, ad spend and filming are discussed separately.

What is Backstage?

White-label production for agencies. Files go out unbranded, the work follows your guidelines, and Nikunj is the contact. Ask if you need an NDA. We agree an overlap in working hours for USA and UAE teams.

Will you publish our name or our work?

Only with your permission. Concept tiles are fictional and labelled. Named client work is shown only when you agree to it.

Which files do we receive?

Final exports on every plan. Editable templates or source files come with Frame, Full Spectrum, and with any project whose scope includes them. The tools are confirmed at the start so they fit your team.

Do you work with teams outside India?

Yes. The studio is in Bhopal. Agency work for the USA and Dubai is remote, with a time-zone overlap we agree before the month starts.
