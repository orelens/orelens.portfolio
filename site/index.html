Process

Four steps. No guesswork.

  1. 1

    Brief

    A free 15-min call. We learn the product, the audience and where the video will live.

  2. 2

    Script & storyboard

    One clear message, written and boarded frame-by-frame for your approval before any animation.

  3. 3

    Design & motion

    We rebuild your UI cleanly and animate it with sound design and pacing that feels premium.

  4. 4

    Deliver & cut down

    Final film plus 16:9, 1:1 and 9:16 versions, ready for site, socials and ads.

FAQ

Straight answers.

What do you need from us to start?

Access to the product (or a staging account / screen recordings), your brand files, and 15 minutes to talk through the goal.

Is my product too early or too complex?

No. We can work from Figma designs before launch, and complex products are exactly where a clear story helps most.

Do you do voiceover and music?

Yes — we handle script, voiceover, music and sound design as part of the film.

How long does a video take?

It depends on length and scope; we agree a timeline on the first call before any work starts.

How much does it cost?

Every project is quoted on scope. Book the free call and you'll get a clear quote afterwards.

Yes. We work remotely over email and WhatsApp with teams anywhere.
