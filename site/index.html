How it works

From brief to delivery

The same path the film ends on, for a single piece or a month of work.

  1. 01

    Brief

    Tell us the brand, what you sell, and where you post. WhatsApp and email both reach Nikunj.

  2. 02

    Concept

    Before a colour is chosen, we ask what the piece has to do, who it is for, and where it will run.

  3. 03

    Design / Edit

    Social creatives, ads, branding, logos, reels and video edits, made as one team.

  4. 04

    Review and revisions

    You review the work. Revisions stay inside the scope we confirmed in writing.

  5. 05

    Delivery

    Final exports go out on the dates we set. If a date slips, we say so before it lands on you.
